Class & Race Features

Hunter's Mark (bonus, L1 slot, conc. 1 min)
Mark a target within 90 ft. +1d6 damage on every weapon hit against it, advantage on Survival/Perception to track. Re-target as a bonus action for free if the marked target drops. Always up.
Colossus Slayer (Hunter, once per turn)
On a hit against a creature below max HP, add +1d8 damage. Triggers from round 2 onward, or any pre-damaged target. Stacks with Hunter's Mark.
Fighting Style — Archery
+2 to attack rolls with ranged weapons. Already baked into the +4 / +7 to-hit numbers below.
Sharpshooter (variant -3/+6)
Power Shot: −3 to hit, +6 damage. Ignore long-range disadvantage (Longbow effective 600 ft). Ignore half & three-quarters cover.
Favored Enemy — Humans & Orcs
Advantage on INT checks to track, WIS checks to recall info. Flavor in PvP; matters only on humanoid map encounters.
Natural Explorer — Forest
Double proficiency on INT/WIS checks tied to forest terrain; travel + foraging bonuses. Map-dependent.
Primeval Awareness (action, 1 slot)
Sense favored creature types within 1 mi (6 mi in forest). Scout utility, not combat.

Signature Moves

1 Mark and Snipe. T1 bonus: Hunter's Mark on priority target. Action: Longbow -3/+6 Sharpshooter at +4 to hit. Round 2+: hit lands while target is wounded → Colossus Slayer +1d8 auto-stacks. Damage: 1d8 + 3 DEX + 6 Sharp + 1d6 HM + 1d8 CS ≈ ~21.5 / hit. Repeats every turn for the full minute.
2 Pull and Kite. Shoot from max range; Sharpshooter ignores long-range disadv, so peel as far as needed. Hunter's Mark transfers free on a kill — never lose tempo. Anti-rush counter to Barbarian / Paladin / Fighter who need to close.
3 Drop Sharpshooter vs. Heavy Armor. Against Paladin (AC 16+) and Fighter (AC 18), drop -3/+6 — the math swings against you. Take the flat +7 to hit, 1d8+3 +HM +CS ≈ ~15.5. Reliable damage beats power damage when miss-rate spikes.
