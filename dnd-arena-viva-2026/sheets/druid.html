Class & Race Features

Combat Wild Shape (bonus action, 2/match)
Transform into a beast (CR 1 cap at L3 thanks to Moon). Bonus action, not full action. Gain the beast's HP as a fresh bar; revert when reduced to 0 or after 1 hr.
Wild Shape Healing (bonus, beast form only)
Spend a spell slot while in beast form: heal 1d8 per slot level. Keeps the bear alive longer without dropping the form.
Spellcasting (WIS)
Spell save DC 13, spell attack +5. Prepares 6 spells/day from the full druid list. Slots: 4× L1, 2× L2.
Mask of the Wild (Wood Elf)
Attempt to Hide when lightly obscured by natural terrain — foliage, rain, mist. Map-dependent; strong on forest/weather maps.
Fleet of Foot · Fey Ancestry
Base speed 35 ft. Advantage on saves vs. Charmed; immune to magical sleep.
Druidic Restriction
No metal armor. Leather + wooden shield only. Don't pick up the Paladin's plate.

Signature Moves

1 Turn-1 Brown Bear. Bonus action: Combat Wild Shape → Brown Bear. Fresh 34 HP pool, claws+bite Multiattack, 40 ft speed. Action: Multiattack — claws (2d6+4) + bite (1d8+4) = ~19 avg dmg. Two-bar tank.
2 Dire Wolf Kite-Lock. Alternative Wild Shape: Dire Wolf — HP 37, AC 14, bite 2d6+3 + STR save DC 11 or prone. Sets up Rogue Sneak Attack and disadvantages enemy ranged. Speed 50 ft.
3 Moonbeam Trap (L2 slot). Druid form. 5 ft column at a chokepoint, CON save DC 13, 2d10 radiant/turn on fail. Move the beam 60 ft as an action on later turns. Sustained zone-denial. Concentration.
