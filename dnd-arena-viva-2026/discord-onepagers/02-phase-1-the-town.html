The Town

You and your two teammates have 60 minutes and 500 gp pooled to prep before the arena. Each move between locations costs 10 minutes ("On to the Next"). Most teams realistically get 3 meaningful stops out of the hour. Choose well.
Clock
60 min
Pooled Gold
500 gp
Per Move
10 min

The Locations — 6 visible · 1 hidden

🏪
General Shop
Potions, oils, basic gear. Haggle (Persuasion DC 15 = −10%, DC 20 = −20%, nat 1 raises prices) or intimidate (DC 18 = −15%, fail = guards called).
📚
The Library
Tactical Study (15 min) — one character gets +2 init, advantage on DEX saves, +15 ft speed. Study an Opponent (10 min) — advantage vs. one enemy early. Copy Spell (30 min, 100 gp, Wizard) — learn a spell.
🎯
Training Grounds
Three drills (15 min, 50 gp, skill check). Master's Forms — +1 attack. Defensive Stance — +1 AC. Bodyguard — reaction to protect an adjacent ally. Plus the Sparring Match (20 min, mini-game) — win it and pick 2 of the 3 buffs.
🗡️
The Black Market
Poisoned Weapon (150 gp, +1d6 on first hit). Stolen Wares (250 gp, pick 1 of 4 magic items — but guards check on every move you make after). Thieves' Tools (100 gp, drop a caltrops mid-match). Steal from the dealer — DC 20, win big or take real damage.
🔮
The Hag's Cottage
10 min, free, three guesses at her riddle. Win = pick one of six boons (hex an enemy, frighten one, read an enemy's HP, resist a damage type, +2 to saves, advantage on death saves). Fail = the time's gone.
🎰
Lucky Dragon Casino
Three games — High Roller (75–200 gp), Card Games (40–100 gp, CHA helps), Dice (20–50 gp). Cheat (DC 18) for an auto-win — fail and you eat damage + fines. Max 2 attempts per team, one per person.
🕳️
Hidden Catacombs  — rumored to lie near one of the six locations
You don't visit the catacombs — you stumble onto them. Every move, your spotter rolls a discovery check. Find them and pick 2 of 3 legendary relics — the biggest prize in town.
House rules. Pay-on-fail — gold's gone if you fail the check. Carry-in damage — town wounds come with you; a 15-min Short Rest heals before the fight.
