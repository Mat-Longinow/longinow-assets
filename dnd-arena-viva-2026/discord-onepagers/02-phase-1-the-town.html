The Town

Where matches are won before a single die is rolled in combat.

You and your two teammates have 60 minutes and 500 gp pooled to prep before the arena. Six visible locations — plus one hidden you'll have to find. Spend smart: there's no banking, every unused minute is a minute set on fire.
Time
60 min
Pooled Gold
500 gp
Locations
6 + 1 hidden

The Locations

🏪
General Shop
Potions, oils, basic gear. Haggle (DC 15 = 10% off, DC 20 = 20%) or intimidate (DC 18 = 15%, fail = guards).
📚
The Library
Research arena history, terrain, or tactics (15 min each, skill-gated). Wizards copy a 1st/2nd-level spell (30 min, 100 gp).
🎯
Training Grounds
Drills for movement, defense, focus, or sparring (15–20 min, 50 gp). Earn small but real combat advantages.
🗡️
Shady Alley
Black market. Poisoned weapons, stolen items, info brokers, trap services. Get caught and you'll lose time and gold.
🔮
The Hag's Cottage
Solve a riddle (10 min, free, 3 guesses). Win = pick one powerful boon. Fail = lose the time.
🎰
Lucky Dragon Casino
High-risk gambling. Card games favor CHA characters. Max 2 attempts per team, one per person.
🕳️
Hidden Catacombs  — secret entrance behind one of the 6 locations
Each search costs 5 min + DC 17 Perception/Investigation. Find it and pick one major reward: an extra spell, a magic item, +250 gp, a potion cache, or arena intel. The biggest prize in town — if you can find it.
The strategic tension. Split up to cover ground or stick together for safety? Spend gold on items, info, or training? Take the riddle gamble or play it safe? Hunt for catacombs (high reward, big time sink) or stick to the visible map? Every minute you spend somewhere is a minute you can't spend somewhere else.
