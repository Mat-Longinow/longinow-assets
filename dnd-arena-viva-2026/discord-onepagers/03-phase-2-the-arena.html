The Arena

3v3 tactical combat. Last team standing wins.

Two teams of three are placed 60 ft apart on the chosen map. Roll initiative (d20 + Dex). No surprise rounds — both teams know each other is there. Then it's blades out.

Round Structure

Round 1
Orient
No turn timer. Read the map, find your angles, set up your opener.
Rounds 2–10
90-Sec Turns
Chess-clock. Ref pauses for rules questions. Move fast or get capped.
Round 11
Sudden Death
All HP drops to 1. Last clash decides it. Still tied? It's a draw.
Victory Condition

Team Deathmatch. First team to drop all three opponents to unconscious wins. A character isn't dead until they fail 3 death saves — being downed is recoverable, but every round on the floor is a round your team is fighting 2v3.

The Locked Rules

  • All characters Level 3 with a +10 HP buffer.
  • No Extra Attack — one swing per Attack action.
  • Sleep is banned (5d8 = team-wipe at L3 HP).
  • Non-combat spells (Tiny Hut, Charm Person) banned.
  • 1-minute spells = whole arena duration.
  • Sharpshooter / GWM: −3 to hit / +6 dmg (not −5/+10).
  • Standard 5e for everything else.
  • Not in the rules? Use your character's abilities.

Combat-Only Skills & Cover

  • Allowed: Grapple/Shove, Hide, Search.
  • Not allowed: Improvised checks, talking your way out, "what if I…" maneuvers.
  • Half cover: +2 AC / +2 Dex saves.
  • Three-quarters: +5 AC / +5 Dex saves.
  • Total cover: can't be targeted.
The referee uses grid squares to settle line-of-sight disputes. Their ruling is final for the rest of the match — and if it's a new situation, it gets logged so future matches handle it the same way.
