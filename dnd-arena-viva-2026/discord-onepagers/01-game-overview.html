Game Overview

D&D Arena is a 3v3 PvP take on D&D 5e. Two teams pick from 12 pre-built characters, prep in a shared town, then meet in tactical combat. Built for drop-in play — a first-timer and a 20-year DM can sit at the same table and both have a good time. Like a team chess match. With fireballs.

The Two Phases

Phase 1

The Town

60 minutes. 500 gp pooled. Shop, train, gamble, gather intel, hunt for hidden catacombs. Every minute is a tradeoff — unused time is lost.

Phase 2

The Arena

3v3 tactical combat. 90-second turns, 10-round cap, sudden death on round 11. Last team standing wins.

How Characters Are Picked

  • 12 pre-built characters — one per PHB class, all Level 3, Arena rules baked in.
  • Each team picks 3 in private, then both teams reveal simultaneously.
  • Mirrors allowed. No counter-picking, no draft order, no bans.

What Winning Looks Like

  • Team Deathmatch. First team to drop all three opponents wins.
  • A character isn't out until 3 failed death saves. Downed is recoverable.
  • Round 10 with no winner? Sudden Death — HP drops to 1, last clash decides.
